MV

I'm Maren — a brand and product designer working with founders who'd rather ship one considered thing than ten forgettable ones. My studio takes on a small number of projects a year, start to finish: strategy, identity, and the interface work that carries it into daily use.

Before going independent I led design at two early-stage product teams, which is where I learned that a system only matters if the people using it every day actually trust it.
